The Mechanism - Video Publisher

A personal tool that uploads my own short videos to my own YouTube channels.

What it does

It writes a script, generates a voice-over, assembles a short vertical video from licensed stock footage, and uploads the finished file to YouTube channels that I own.

Why it needs YouTube access

To upload videos, to set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and to read the list of videos already published so it does not post duplicates.

Who can use it

Only the owner of this project. There is no sign-up, no other users and no public interface.
